If you installed Windows 11 and you are like me, then you don't like the new Notepad and Paint. 

I extract them out from Windows 7, and it turns out, they work perfectly fine on Windows 11. 

You can download them from here: https://blog.mdavid626.com/assets/windows7_notepad_mspaint_calc.zip

You could extract the zip into a folder and run them from there, or just place them into "C:\Windows\system32". Don't forget to copy also the .mui files (in the en-US folder). You might need to disable "App aliases" in Settings -> Apps -> Advanced App Settings -> App execution aliases.
